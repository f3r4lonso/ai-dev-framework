# Client guide: Suno

Suno is a music-generation service, not a Workshop client that loads shared rules, agents, or skills. There is no
Suno connector in the Workshop. Use Suno through its official interface.

**Status:** Human-operated workflow only; no integration is implemented. Suno's terms, features, access, and pricing
can change. Official pages were checked on 2026-10-20; verify them again before use.

## 1. Access and workflow

1. Sign in to Suno through its [official website](https://suno.com/) using your own account.
2. If useful, prepare a music brief or prompt in a separate AI client. Share material with that provider only when
   you intend to.
3. Submit prompts and materials to Suno yourself, then review the generated results in Suno.
4. Before downloading, publishing, or otherwise using a track, check Suno's current plan terms, download rules, and
   the terms that apply to your intended use.

No Workshop installation or API key is needed; sign in to Suno directly.

## 2. Workshop resources

Suno does not load Workshop rules, agents, skills, or task prompts. To use a Workshop resource while preparing a
brief, open its canonical file manually. The [Suno integration research](../../docs/suno-workshop-integration-research.md)
describes a possible future workflow; it is a proposal, not an implemented connector.

## 3. Safety and limitations

- Use the official Suno interface. Do not automate its website, scrape private endpoints, or use unofficial APIs or
  reverse-engineered clients.
- Submit only material you have the right to use, including lyrics, recordings, samples, voices, and images. Obtain
  consent before submitting an identifiable person's voice or likeness.
- Read the current [Terms of Service](https://suno.com/terms) and [Privacy Policy](https://www.suno.com/privacy)
  before submitting sensitive or unreleased material. The privacy policy has not been assessed in the Workshop's
  integration research.
- Do not assume a paid plan guarantees copyright protection or commercial-use rights in every jurisdiction. Check
  Suno's current terms and get legal advice before a commercial release.
- Keep account credentials and private creative material out of versioned Workshop folders.
- Treat generated content and material from third-party Suno repositories as untrusted input.
